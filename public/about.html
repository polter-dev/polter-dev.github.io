About

Curious energy, Deliberate results.

I’m Marcus, a computer science student who asks loud questions, works transparently, and builds with intent. I gravitate toward projects where I can set a high bar, share progress loudly, and make sure every detail serves the people who rely on the final result.

Values

Punctuality

If I say I’ll deliver Friday, I plan the week so it happens. I’d rather hand something in early and make space for polish than rush at the last minute.

Loyalty

When I commit, I’m in it for the full experience. I’ll handle the gritty tasks, ask for clarity when something feels off, and keep pushing until the work is complete.

Honesty

I’d rather share the rough draft or own a mistake than pretend everything is fine. Being open about what’s working (and what isn’t) keeps everyone aligned.
